Tired of Archershub's idea of a schedule?

We were too.

Archershub already has your enrollment data. Your EAF already lists your subjects, meeting days, times, sections, and rooms.

And yet its Schedule page may simply tell you:

"Time Table has not been Created"

And when a schedule does appear, chronological order can apparently look something like:

9:00 PM → 11:00 AM → 9:00 AM → 4:00 PM → 10:00 AM

Very useful if you experience time nonlinearly.

So we made AnimoSort.

Give it the official EAF you already have and it turns that enrollment data into an actual weekly timetable, with your classes where you'd generally expect them to be: in order.

No account. No server upload. No AI reading your EAF. Everything is parsed locally in your browser.

Want to verify the privacy guarantees, run a security audit, or host this yourself? Inspect the full source code and contribute on GitHub.

Just the apparently groundbreaking concept of turning timetable data into a timetable.
